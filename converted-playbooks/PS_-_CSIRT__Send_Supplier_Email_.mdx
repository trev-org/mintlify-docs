    | Complete After | 1 reply/replies |

    </Accordion>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide Vendor's Name" icon="clipboard-list">
    <Info>
    **Data Collection:** This task gathers information from security analysts.
    </Info>

    **Form Title:** Vendor Name

    <Accordion title="Form Questions (1)" icon="clipboard-question">

    **1. Please provide the Vendor's Name** **[Required]**

    - **Type:** `shortText`

    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Collection Settings" icon="gear">

    | Setting | Value |
    |---------|-------|
    | Retry Attempts | 2 |
    | Retry Interval | 360 seconds |
    | Complete After | 1 reply/replies |

    </Accordion>

  </Step>
  <Step title="PS - Sub playbook: Format Incident Owner Name" icon="diagram-project">
    <Tip>
    **Calls Sub-Playbook:** `PS - Sub playbook: Format Incident Owner Name`
    
    This sub-playbook runs in an isolated context. Data must be explicitly passed via inputs and outputs.
    </Tip>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Reformat Vendor Name" icon="user">
    <Warning>
    **Manual Task:** This step requires human intervention and decision-making.
    </Warning>

    Set a value in context under the key you entered.

    **Executes Script:** `Set`

    <Accordion title="Script Arguments (2)" icon="code">

    | Argument | Value |
    |----------|-------|
    | `key` | `${Vendor Name.Answers.0}` |
    | `value` | `Vendor Name.Answers.0` → `replace(limit={})` → `replace(limit={})` |

    </Accordion>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Supplier Email" icon="terminal">
    Sends an email.

    **Executes Command:** `!send-mail`
    **Integration:** EWSO365_testing
    **Full Path:** `EWSO365_testing|||send-mail`

    <Accordion title="Command Arguments (3)" icon="terminal">

    | Argument | Value |
    |----------|-------|
    | `body` | `Hi,  My name is ${incident.incidentowner}, and I am a part of the Mondelez Cybersecurity Incident Response Team (CSIRT). During routine monitoring, it was identified that files related to Mondelez and ${Vendor Name.Answers.0} operations have been leaked on a dedicated leak site.  In order to get a full scope of understanding, we have prepared the following list of questions we would like answered. These questions will help us determine if there is any concern or impact to MDLZ data and/or MDLZ systems.  Please feel free to forward these questions to any Security or IT Points of Contact (POCs), or to include them on this email thread.  1.	Was ${Vendor Name.Answers.0} victim of a cyber-attack and compromise? 2.	If so, what is the nature and scope of the incident?  3.	Has the root cause been identified? If so, please share.  4.	When was the incident discovered and reported?  5.	What are the potential risks and impacts on your organization and ours?  6.	Have affected accounts been reset?   7.	Are affected accounts being monitored?  8.	What steps have been taken to contain the incident? How will King Power Corporation handle incident response?  9.	How have you confirmed that an attacker was not able to move to another system in your environment or ones closely connected to yours?  10.	Are affected hosts being reimaged? If not, what steps have been taken to ensure they are no longer compromised?  11.	Was MDLZ data present or accessible to attackers at the time of compromise?  12.	Is there evidence that data was exfiltrated?  13.	How much MDLZ data was exfiltrated?   14.	Can we have a copy of our data for review?  15.	If there is an incident report available (or to be available in the future), can we have a copy?  a.	We would like a report to validate or provide a statement that the incident has been contained.  16.	What future safeguards are being put in place to protect MDLZ data in the future?  a.	Additional security endpoints?  b.	Establishment of a SOC?    We look forward to your responses.  Thank you, ${incident.incidentowner} Cybersecurity Incident Response Analyst Global MDS Cybersecurity Program` |
    | `subject` | `${Vendor Name.Answers.0} Leaked Files` |
    | `to` | `${Provide Supplier Email.Answers.0}` |

    </Accordion>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Today's Date" icon="user">
    <Warning>
    **Manual Task:** This step requires human intervention and decision-making.
    </Warning>

    Retrieves the current date and time.

    **Executes Script:** `GetTime`

  </Step>
  <Step title="Format Today's Date" icon="user">
    <Warning>
    **Manual Task:** This step requires human intervention and decision-making.
    </Warning>

    **Executes Script:** `ConvertXSOARDatetoEWSDate`

    <Accordion title="Script Arguments (1)" icon="code">

    | Argument | Value |
    |----------|-------|
    | `TodaysDate` | `${TimeNow}` |

    </Accordion>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Supplier Email to List" icon="terminal">
    commands.local.cmd.list.add

    **Executes Command:** `!addToList`
    **Full Path:** `Builtin|||addToList`

    <Accordion title="Command Arguments (2)" icon="terminal">

    | Argument | Value |
    |----------|-------|
    | `listData` | `{${Provide Supplier Email.Answers.0}: ${Formatted Todays Date}}` |
    | `listName` | `CSIRT Suppliers Email Communication` |

    </Accordion>

  </Step>
